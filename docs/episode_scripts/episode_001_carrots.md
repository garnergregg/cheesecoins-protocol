# Episode 001 — *Where Did All The Carrots Go?*

**Series:** Annie + Alice on Money
**Topic:** Inflation
**Format:** TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts (vertical 9:16, 2 minutes)
**Style:** Warm, funny, lands one idea cleanly

---

## Cast

- **Bramble** — kid goat, indignant, dramatic, the lead questioner
- **Pip** — kid goat, sweet, literal
- **Clover** — kid goat, the one who connects the dots out loud
- **Aunt Annie** — adult goat, calm, knowing, gentle teacher
- **Aunt Alice** — adult goat, drier, sharper, the comedy beat
- **Farmer Greg** — off-camera voice only, bookends the episode

## Visuals (b-roll Greg has or can shoot)

- Wide shot of kid goats around a feed bowl, fewer carrots than last year
- Tight close-up on Bramble's confused face
- Aunt Annie + Aunt Alice walking up the path
- Brief illustrated insert: same bowl, "last summer 10 carrots, today 8"
- Carrot patch / fresh greens on the actual farm
- Closing wide of all the goats together, fresh carrots scattered

---

## Script

### COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:15

*[Wide shot, kid goats around a bowl with only a few carrots in it]*

**BRAMBLE** *(indignant)*: Wait. Wait wait wait.

*[Tight on the bowl]*

**BRAMBLE**: Last summer this bowl was FULL of carrots. Today it's, like, half. What happened?

**PIP**: Maybe Farmer Greg forgot.

**CLOVER**: Farmer Greg never forgets carrots.

*[Cut — Aunt Annie and Aunt Alice approach]*

---

### SETUP — 0:15–0:35

**BRAMBLE** *(running up)*: Aunt Annie! Aunt Alice! Why are there fewer carrots than last summer?!

*[Aunt Annie and Aunt Alice exchange a knowing look]*

**AUNT ANNIE** *(calmly)*: Ah. That's a big question, little one.

**AUNT ALICE** *(dry)*: Buckle up.

---

### THE LESSON — 0:35–1:30

**AUNT ANNIE**: The carrots aren't gone. The bowl is the same size. There are just fewer carrots in it for the same number of pellets you traded.

*[Visual: illustrated graphic — same bowl, last summer 10 carrots, this summer 8]*

**PIP**: But I gave the same number of pellets!

**AUNT ALICE**: Yes. Pellets buy fewer carrots than they used to. That's what happens to "money" when there's more of it floating around than there are carrots.

**CLOVER** *(slowly, working it out)*: More pellets... fewer carrots... so each pellet is worth less?

**AUNT ANNIE**: Exactly. Humans have a name for it. They call it *inflation*.

**BRAMBLE**: That's not fair.

**AUNT ALICE**: No. It's not.

*[Beat]*

**BRAMBLE**: So what do we do about it?

**AUNT ANNIE**: You start by understanding it. Then you ask the next question: what would actually keep the bowl full?

*[Cut to footage of the actual farm — carrot patch, goats eating fresh greens]*

**AUNT ANNIE** *(gentler)*: The carrots didn't shrink. The pellets did. Carrots are real. Pellets are a story we agreed to tell. Real things — soil, water, kid goats, kid humans — those don't disappear when someone prints more pellets.

---

### THE META-JOKE — 1:30–1:50

**PIP** *(suspicious)*: Aunt Annie. How do you guys even know all this?

**AUNT ALICE** *(deadpan)*: Farmer Greg.

**AUNT ANNIE**: For ten years.

**AUNT ALICE**: At the fence. Every morning.

**AUNT ANNIE**: While he's milking us.

**AUNT ALICE**: He calls it ranting. We call it free education.

*[Quick audio clip: Farmer Greg's actual voice from behind camera, mid-rant]*

**FARMER GREG** *(off-camera, audio only)*: — and another thing about the money supply —

**ALL KID GOATS** *(in unison)*: Ohhhhhh.

---

### CLOSE — 1:50–2:00

*[Wide shot — Farmer Greg off-camera scatters fresh carrots from the patch into the bowl]*

**FARMER GREG** *(off-camera)*: Eat up, girls.

**BRAMBLE** *(mouth full)*: Aunt Annie? What ARE pellets actually *backed* by, anyway?

**AUNT ANNIE**: Ohhh. That's next week.

*[Title card: **NEXT: What Are Pellets Even MADE Of?**]*

*[Cheesecoins / Annie + Alice handle out]*

## Production notes

- **Voices:** can be Hedra-generated (already in production pipeline for Annie + Alice) or voice-acted directly. The kid goat voices are the new asset — needs distinct young voices (Hedra portrait gen + ElevenLabs young-voice presets work cleanly here).
- **Visual style:** mostly real footage with a brief illustrated/animated insert for the "10 carrots vs 8 carrots" beat. Keeps production cost low and the documentary feel intact.
- **Farmer Greg's voice:** keep it real — pull a 4-second audio clip from any actual farm-side rant. Authenticity is the joke.
- **Length:** 2 minutes is the TikTok / Reels sweet spot. YouTube Shorts caps at 60 seconds — for that channel, cut the lesson section tighter and lose the meta-joke beat (or keep meta-joke and tighten the lesson).

## Why this episode works

- Lands ONE idea (inflation) without naming it until the kid goats have intuitively understood it
- Uses concrete, visible, edible objects (carrots, pellets) that a 10-year-old already understands
- The "Farmer Greg has been ranting for 10 years" beat is the kind of meta-joke that makes adults laugh and gives kids permission to ask the same questions
- Closes with a cliffhanger that pulls the audience to Episode 002 ("what ARE pellets backed by?") — building the series through curiosity, not algorithm
- The kid goats drive the questions; Annie + Alice answer warmly. No lecture. The lesson rides on genuine curiosity
