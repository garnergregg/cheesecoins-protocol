Section 6

Commerce NFT Lifecycle

A Commerce NFT is a forward commitment. The issuer promises to deliver. The holder carries the on-chain receipt. Every state transition is permanent — there is no reversing a fulfillment record.

Buyer purchases NFT Payment collected in CURD · NFT minted · sale recorded on-chain
↓
PENDING NFT held by buyer · delivery expected by maturityDate
↓ three possible outcomes ↓
FULFILLED Issuer calls markFulfilled(tokenId) Delivery confirmed on-chain · permanent record · NFT retains fulfilled status forever
CANCELLED Issuer calls markCancelled(tokenId) Cancellation recorded on-chain · refund reference stored · issuer responsible for refund off-chain
EXPIRED maturityDate passes without delivery Parties resolve off-chain or via governance dispute · on-chain record shows expired state

Section 8

US Treasuries and T-bill Reserve

The protocol holds tokenized US Treasuries as a reserve asset. This yield supplements staking rewards and backs a standalone Treasury Yield NFT for conservative investors who want yield without farm exposure.

Protocol Reserve Layer
  • USDC from NFT sales and CURD purchases flows to Treasury
  • Protocol allocates a portion to tokenized T-bills
  • Example providers: Ondo USDY, Superstate USTB on Arbitrum One
  • ~4.5% APY pass-through to the protocol
→
Yield Distribution
  • T-bill yield accrues quarterly
  • Flows into BootstrapYieldPool
  • Distributed to staked CURD holders
  • Distributed to Scene NFT stakers as supplement
  • Paid to Treasury Yield NFT holders as fixed quarterly payout
Treasury Yield NFT Future product — not yet deployed
  • Buyer purchases NFT for a fixed USDC amount (e.g. 500 USDC)
  • Protocol allocates to T-bill reserve position
  • Holder receives ~4.5% APY quarterly in USDC or CURD
  • Redeemable at maturity or tradeable on secondary market
CURD Token Backing
  • Agricultural real-world assets — farm NFTs, CSA revenue, land
  • US Treasury reserve — tokenized T-bills on-chain
  • Uniswap liquidity pool — CURD/USDC (Phase B, needs pool first)
CURD is utility-pegged — real goods are priced in CURD, so the price is held by real demand for real food. Not an algorithm. Not a bank. Banks hate this.
